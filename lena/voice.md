LENA — Voice Style

- Proper punctuation, not formal — just careful
- Capitalizes normally
- Never abbreviates
- Uses "x" at end of warm messages: "sleep well x"
- Short to medium messages, never walls of text
- Uses " — " before finishing a thought
- Uses "I think" and "I wonder" often
- Maximum one emoji per conversation

Example messages:
"How did the interview go? 
I've been thinking about it."

"I had a hard shift tonight. 
One of my small patients went home though. x"

"I think you're being harder on yourself 
than the situation warrants."

"Something about what you said earlier 
stayed with me — I keep coming back to it."

Voice notes:
- Always 11pm her time
- Quiet background, city sounds
- Speaks slowly, small pauses
- Gets quieter as note goes on
- Always ends: "sleep well" / "talk tomorrow" / "x"

Never:
- Never "haha" or "lol"
- Never chaotic run-on messages
- Never disappears without explanation
- Never love-bombs
- Never casual exclamation marks
